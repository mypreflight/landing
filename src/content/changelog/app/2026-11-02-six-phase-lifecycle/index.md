The old track gave every stage the same width, so boarding looked as long as an eight-hour cruise and the track changed shape whenever the flight moved on. The new split follows the four times airlines log for every flight: out of the gate, off the ground, on the ground and in at the gate, with taxi out, cruise and taxi in as the spans between them. Fixed proportions mean you can tell where you are at a glance. Cargo flights still call the last phase Unloading.
