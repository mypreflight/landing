Flight history used to be a table with a preview of one flight beside it, so you only ever saw one route at a time. Now the routes you fly sit together on the same globe, the ones you fly often stand out, and a flight with no recorded track still shows as its great circle, dashed. Opening a flight keeps the globe in view instead of starting a new page from blank, and going back returns you to the same place in the list.
