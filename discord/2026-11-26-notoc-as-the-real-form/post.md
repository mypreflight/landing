**Your NOTOC is now the real form, and it downloads as one!**

The NOTOC reads like the form a captain is handed and downloads as that form in PDF. The OFP and runway analysis open with their issue time and download SimBrief's own PDF.

- **The real form**: the header, dangerous goods by the form's columns, special loads with their handling, special category passengers and the certification. Cold chain and the load by compartment moved to the loadsheet.
- **Fold to one line**: each section shows how many entries it holds and folds to a line of codes once you've read it.
- **Download PDF**: a landscape A4 form, column for column like the paper one, that prints whole and continues onto further sheets.
- **OFP**: opens with its release and issue time, runs across the whole panel, and Download PDF saves SimBrief's own file.
- **Runway analysis**: the same summary and the same SimBrief PDF.
