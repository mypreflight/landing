**Import your own SimBrief plan and fly it straight away!**

No more waiting for Operations: import the latest plan from your SimBrief account right on the dashboard, and you're checked in with its estimated times, with the briefing ready.

- **Import from SimBrief**: see the route, the scheduled times with the block time and your aircraft before you import.
- **Your dashboard, your flights**: Recent flights, Flight history and Next scheduled flight show only your own flights, and a new pilot adds a SimBrief ID right on the dashboard.
- **Departure stand**: still yours to pick after check-in, until boarding starts.
- **Unflown plans**: a plan you import but don't board within 24 hours is removed, so you can import the next one.
