Until now the vertical profile appeared only once a flight was over. In flight you had this minute's altitude and speed, and nothing to show how the climb went or how far you were from the plan's top of descent. Now the profile builds as you fly, with the dashed plan running on to landing, and pointing at any minute moves the aircraft on the map to where you were then. On a long flight the whole profile in one chart leaves the climb and the descent a few pixels wide, so zoom in, down to five minutes, and every step climb and level-off gets room of its own.
