Long-haul routes spend hours far north, exactly where a flat map stretches the most, so the shortest path between two airports looked like a detour. On a round earth, distances and the shape of the route read the way you fly them. Every map, flat or round, now uses one basemap, VersaTiles Positrino, with English labels in both themes.
