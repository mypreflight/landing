The NOTOC used to be a summary of the loads, with cold chain advice and a load breakdown that no captain's form carries. Now it shows what the form a captain is handed shows: the dangerous goods row by row with their UN numbers and positions, the special loads with the temperatures to keep them at, and the special category passengers, down to where a wheelchair's battery travels. Crews are given the NOTOC before departure and sign it to accept the load, and the PDF is laid out as that form, so you can print it, read it in the cockpit and sign it. The cold chain assessments and the load in each compartment have moved to the loadsheet, where you read the load.

The OFP and runway analysis open the same way, with the release and the time SimBrief issued it, and Download PDF saves SimBrief's own PDF of your plan. The plan now runs across the whole panel instead of sitting small in a box of its own.
