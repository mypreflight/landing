**See which runways the ATIS has in use, right next to yours!**

The runway card and the runway picker now read the ATIS for you: the runways in use for your departure or arrival, with the information letter, and the wind along and across each runway.

- **Runway card**: a green ATIS chip when the ATIS uses your runway, an amber one naming the runway it uses instead, and a wind chip with the headwind or tailwind and the crosswind.
- **Runway picker**: ARR and DEP on every runway in use, a wind chip on every row, and the ATIS source, time and wind at the top.
- **ATIS source**: SayIntentions AI, or Real, the newer of the FAA and atis.guru ATIS. Switch in the briefing, or set your default in Settings.
