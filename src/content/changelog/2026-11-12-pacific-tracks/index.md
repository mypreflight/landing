Flights between Asia and North America cross the Pacific on organised tracks that Oakland publishes for westbound traffic and Fukuoka for eastbound, but none of them showed on the map. Both sets are laid out fresh each day around the jet stream, so seeing them next to your own route shows how today's traffic flows across the ocean. Only the tracks in force right now are drawn, and the next set appears on its own when it takes over.
