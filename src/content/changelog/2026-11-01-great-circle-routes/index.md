On a flat map a straight line isn't the shortest path, and the gap grows the further north you fly. Long-haul flights follow great circles, bent by winds and airspace, which is why a flight from New York to Hong Kong passes over the Arctic. The lines now show the path you actually fly, and a rotation leg across the date line draws in full.
