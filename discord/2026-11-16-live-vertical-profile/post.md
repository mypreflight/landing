**MyPreflight 2.133.0: your vertical profile, live and zoomable**

The vertical profile now sits under the map on the Tracking view, so you can watch the climb, cruise and descent build against the plan while you fly.

- **Live profile**: your altitude and ground speed so far over the plan's altitude to landing, with the latest reading beside it. Point at any minute and the aircraft on the map moves there.
- **Zoom**: scroll or pinch over the profile, or use the zoom buttons, to zoom into any part of the flight, down to five minutes, and drag to move along it. It works in flight history too.
- **Recent flights**: the dashboard shows your last three flights instead of one, each a click from its flight history.

The dashboard greeting and a finished flight's details now sit in the top bar.
