Flights between Europe and North America cross the Atlantic on organised tracks that Shanwick publishes for the westbound daytime flow and Gander for the eastbound night flow, laid out fresh each day around the jet stream, but none of them showed on the map. Crews request their oceanic clearance for a track and a level before they reach the entry point, so seeing the day's tracks next to your filed route shows the one you're planned on and the ones running beside it. The North Atlantic and Pacific tracks have a row each, so you can show the ocean you're crossing and leave the other off. Only the tracks in force right now are drawn, and the next set appears on its own when it takes over.
