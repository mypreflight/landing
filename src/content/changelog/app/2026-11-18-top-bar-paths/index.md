Titles and paths used to take the first lines of every page, with that page's buttons beside them and a back button on the create pages. Now the content starts right under the top bar, and every step of the path takes you back up a level: an aircraft reads Operators › American Airlines › Fleet › N767CX, an airport's runways Airports › CYQX › Runways, and a flight starts with the list that holds it, whether Flight plans, Current flights or Flight history. Cabin crew pages do the same, from Statistics and Travel log to Rotations › BOS-PHL-BOS 2026-01-01 and Airports library › CYYR › Gates. On a phone, the title stays at the top of the page as before.
