**Each airport on one card, with stands and runways picked on the map!**

Preflight and Arrival now open with one card per airport: the METAR, TAF and ATIS beside your parking stand and runway, in the order the aircraft meets them.

- **Airport card**: the stand with its terminal, boarding, guidance, services and its position for aligning the IRS, and the runway with its heading, length, width and elevation.
- **Stand picker**: search by name, terminal or type, or click a stand on the airport map. Pick one from the list and the map glides to it.
- **Runway picker**: every runway on the map, with the departure path or the approach drawn from the one you pick.
