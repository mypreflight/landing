The briefing used to sort everything by kind of data, so the departure airport turned up in three tabs and nothing said which tab mattered now. Airline crews brief in the order the flight happens, a departure briefing before they push and an approach briefing before top of descent, and the Briefing view now follows that order. Before departure you get the weather at both ends, your runway and stand, fuel, load, passengers and crew on one page; over the ocean, the track and your fuel at each fix; for the arrival, the destination weather, your runway and stand, the landing figures and the alternates. Finished phases are marked done, and the long documents open beside the page instead of replacing it, so you don't lose your place.
